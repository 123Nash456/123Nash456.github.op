Work sample · ie360 UAT Test Plan V1.1

Testing the platform the way clients use it

In October 2026 I rebuilt the user acceptance test plan against the live platform, tab by tab from Home to Help centre. Admin and CRM screens stayed out of scope; the only tests there confirm clients can’t reach them.

Step 1

Walk the live product

Every sidebar area, as an owner, learner and broker would see it.

Step 2

Write the cases

Pre-conditions, steps and expected results, in sidebar order.

Step 3

Track the change

Each case marked New, Updated, Moved or Unchanged against V1.0.

Step 4

Log findings

Issues seen on the live platform, rated by severity and tied to a test.

Step 5

Report readiness

Auto-counting summary, a briefing deck and a client feedback survey.
